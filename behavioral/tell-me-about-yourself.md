# Tell me about yourself

> The filled, personal version lives in `personal_tell-me-about-yourself.md` —
> gitignored (`personal_*`), like all files carrying real personal content.

- **Length target:** 90 seconds spoken ≈ 220 words. Time it out loud; written versions
  always run long.
- **Arc:** past → present → why here. The first three blocks are fixed once written;
  only the **bridge** swaps per company/role.
- **Rule:** every sentence either moves the arc forward or gets cut. No resume recitation.

## The spine

### 1. Hook — one sentence, ~10s

Who you are professionally, in one line a stranger could repeat back.

> `[FILL — e.g. "I'm a <role> who <the one thing that makes you distinct>."]`

*Guiding question: if the interviewer remembered exactly one sentence about you, which one?*

### 2. Past — two chapters max, ~30s

Not a chronology — pick the 1–2 moves that explain how you got here. Each chapter is one
sentence of what + one of "so what" that points toward the present.

> **Chapter 1:** `[FILL — where you started and the skill/interest it planted]`
>
> **Chapter 2 (optional):** `[FILL — the turn or decision that redirected you toward what
> you do now]`

*Guiding questions: what did you study / where did you start, and what pulled you from
there toward your current work? What decision would you defend if asked "why did you
leave X"?*

### 3. Present — current role + one flagship result, ~30s

Current role in one sentence, then the single strongest thing you've done there, with a
number. This is the story the interviewer will pull on first — pick one that has a full
STAR file behind it in [stories/](stories/).

> `[FILL — current role, scope]`
>
> `[FILL — flagship result with a number, e.g. "most recently I <did X>, which <moved
> metric Y by Z>"]`

*Guiding question: which of your stories do you most want the follow-up questions to
land on?*

### 4. Bridge — why here, ~20s (SWAPPABLE)

The only block that changes per interview. One sentence connecting your trajectory to
*this* role, one concrete detail about *them* that a competitor wouldn't satisfy.
Maintained per firm in [company-notes/](company-notes/) — write the variant there and
rehearse from it.

> Default shape: "That's what points me at `<this role>` — `[FILL: the specific thing
> about their problem/desk/team that your trajectory sets up]`."

## Compressions

- **60-second version:** cut Chapter 1; hook → chapter 2 → present → bridge.
- **30-second version ("quick intro" in a panel):** hook + present + one-line bridge.

## Delivery notes

- Stop at the end of the bridge. Silence is the interviewer's cue, not yours to fill.
- First person singular for everything you did; "we" only for context.
- Don't repeat what's on the resume they're holding — interpret it.
- The last sentence should hand them an easy follow-up you *want* to answer.
